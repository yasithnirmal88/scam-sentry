### SLIDE 3: The Big Question — Why Conventional AI Models Fail
* **Visual Style:** A "VS" (Versus) comparison layout. Left side: Red ❌ (Conventional AI). Right side: Green ✔️ (Scam Sentry).
* **Canva Elements to Search:** `versus comparison layout`, `broken robot icon`, `magnifying glass inspection`.

#### On-Slide Content:
* **Header:** **The 'Shortcut Learning' Trap: Why Pure ML Gets Fooled**
* **Subtitle:** *Standard models memorize word patterns instead of real scam mechanics.*
* **Comparison Columns:**
  * ❌ **Standard Pure ML (Bag-of-Words / TF-IDF only):**
    * Penalizes genuine words: Words like *"Assistant"*, *"Entry-level"*, or *"Flexible"* get labeled FAKE because of dataset imbalances.
    * Misses smart scams: A well-written corporate scam asking for a wire deposit gets labeled REAL because it uses fancy words!
  * ✔️ **Scam Sentry (Hybrid Approach):**
    * Combines Machine Learning **+** 13 Hand-Engineered Fraud Mechanics.
    * Never flags normal words. Flags the actual crime: Upfront fees, suspicious channels, and unrealistic claims.

#### 🗣️ Natural Speaker Script:
> *"You might ask: 'Why not just throw a standard Machine Learning model at the text?' Here's the trap we discovered in our early research: Standard models memorize word frequencies. If a training dataset has lots of fake ads for 'Data Entry Assistant', the AI learns that the word 'Assistant' equals fake! Meanwhile, an executive-sounding scam that politely asks for money flies under the radar. To solve this, we built a hybrid AI system."*

#### On-Slide Content:

* **Subtitle:** *Handling noisy screenshots and scanned flyers without manual typing.*
* **Visual Branching:**
  * 📄 **Digital Vector PDFs** ➔ Extracted instantly via character maps (`pdfplumber`).
  * 🖼️ **Scanned / Image PDFs** ➔ Character count < 30?

    * 🔍 **Tesseract OCR Engine:** Scans text lines automatically.
  * 📸 **Social Media Screenshots (PNG/JPG)** ➔ Direct 24-bit RGB sanitization and Tesseract character parsing.

#### 🗣️ Natural Speaker Script:
> *"Real-world job postings aren't just typed text; they are WhatsApp flyers and Canva posters shared on Instagram. A common failure in naive PDF tools is that scanned PDFs return blank text. We solved this with an intelligent fallback threshold: if direct text extraction yields under 30 characters, Scam Sentry automatically rasterizes the document at 200 DPI and invokes Tesseract OCR. This ensures 100% extraction success."*

### SLIDE 8: Secret Weapon 3 — Gradient Boosted Trees & Training
* **Visual Style:** Split layout showing Decision Trees graphic on one side and Dataset Strategy on the other.
* **Canva Elements to Search:** `decision tree algorithm`, `machine learning training graphic`, `dataset chart`.

#### On-Slide Content:
* **Header:** **The AI Brain: Gradient Boosted Trees & Dataset Balance**
* **Subtitle:** *Learning real deception patterns, not superficial job titles.*
* **Key Highlights:**
  * 🌲 **The Classifier:** `GradientBoostingClassifier` (150 decision trees, learning rate 0.1, depth 4). Drop-in compatible with XGBoost.
  * 🧩 **Matrix Union:** Merges 2,500 TF-IDF N-grams with the dense 13-rule vector via `scipy.sparse.hstack`.
  * ⚖️ **Synthetic Balanced Training:** Deliberately balances identical job titles across both Genuine and Fake classes to prevent title bias.
  * 🌐 **Kaggle EMSCAD Ready:** One-line command flag (`--real`) integrates 17,880 real-world job postings from the Aegean dataset.

#### 🗣️ Natural Speaker Script:
> *"At the heart of Scam Sentry is an ensemble of 150 Gradient Boosted Decision Trees. We combine 2,500 unigram and bigram text features with our 13 rule signals. Crucially, our synthetic training dataset distributes titles like 'Assistant' and 'Manager' equally between real and fake ads. This forces the model to evaluate the true substance of the offer rather than taking shortcuts based on job titles."*

### SLIDE 13: Engineering Roadblocks Overcome
* **Visual Style:** 3 Challenge ➔ Solution cards with wrench/tools icons.
* **Canva Elements to Search:** `problem solution card`, `gear tools icon`, `bug fix tech icon`.

#### On-Slide Content:
* **Header:** **Real-World Engineering: Challenges & Lessons Learned**
* **Subtitle:** *Overcoming runtime obstacles during development (Sections 12-13 of Progress Report).*
* **3 Roadblocks Solved:**
  1. ⚙️ **Python 3.13 Pandas Build Failure:** Older pandas lacked pre-compiled C wheels for Python 3.13. Upgraded to `pandas >= 3.0.0` with native binary compatibility.
  2. 🔄 **Pickle Drift & Feature Mismatches:** Changing feature counts invalidated older `.pkl` files. Engineered an automated model retraining pipeline to keep artifacts synchronized.
  3. 🔍 **Windows Tesseract Binary Discovery:** Added intelligent auto-discovery in `ocr_utils.py` targeting `C:\Program Files\Tesseract-OCR` when missing from system PATH.

#### 🗣️ Natural Speaker Script:
> *"Real software engineering involves overcoming unexpected roadblocks. During initial setup, Pandas failed to build on Python 3.13 due to missing C wheels — solved by upgrading to Pandas 3.0. Later, updating our fraud features caused pickle shape mismatches — solved by building an automated retraining script. Finally, missing Tesseract paths on Windows were resolved with an auto-discovery locator."*
